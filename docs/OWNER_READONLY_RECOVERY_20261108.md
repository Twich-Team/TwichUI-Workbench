# Standing owner decision: read-only investigation recovery

On November 8, 2026 Rich independently authorized this standing policy. It replaces
separate quarantine exceptions for every qualifying investigation, without approving
unspecified provisions of the earlier proposal.

Workbench may quarantine uncertain investigation/planning and start a fresh
investigation without another owner decision only after verifying:

- The operation had no capability to modify source, publish, perform consequential
  external actions, or dispatch implementation.
- Old output cannot become an approved plan or trigger execution.
- Continuing activity and late responses are isolated from the new task and cannot
  affect its state or approvals.
- Supported status retrieval and cancellation have been reasonably investigated
  and used where appropriate.
- Fresh work has valid task bindings and existing disclosure, access and billing
  permissions. No old approval or partial output is reused.

Verify actual capabilities and isolation; absence of an observed write is insufficient.
Qualifying read-only generation may have consumed included allowance without a final
receipt. Preserve final outcome and usage as unknown; never invent zero usage,
successful cancellation or completion. This is not authority for paid charges,
credits, reload, uncertain writes, publication or consequential external side effects.

Evaluate the newer whitespace incident under this policy, record evidence and
quarantine it if eligible. Repair ordinary isolation/recovery defects autonomously.
Ask only when actual consequential uncertainty or unavailable capability remains.

Fix lifecycle handling rather than relying on repeated quarantines. Content failure
does not establish execution state. Retain supported thread/turn identities and
bounded private evidence before parsing; preserve lifecycle notifications separately.
Handle malformed output, disconnects, timeouts, late completion, cancellation and
restart. Preserve recoverable identities and prevent old results gaining authority.
Use supported provider mechanisms, never credential inspection or invented access.
Test faults, then exercise the real integration with a meaningful correction.

Implement this in recovery decisions and persistent guidance. Preserve historical
incidents, unknown usage, source, credentials, billing, network, independent checks,
recovery and publication safeguards. Complete the real checked repair, provider
checks, installed-result recovery, next ordinary bug and remaining agreed scope.
No campaign clock, allocation, revision ceiling or repeated renewal applies.
