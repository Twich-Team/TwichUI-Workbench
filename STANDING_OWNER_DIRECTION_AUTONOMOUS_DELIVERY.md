# Standing Owner Direction: Autonomous Delivery

Updated: November 7, 2026
Owner: Rich

## Purpose and precedence

TwichUI Workbench is a personal project. Deliver a useful working product and substantial connected progress, not enterprise-scale process, elaborate approval machinery, or excessive documentation.

This is standing authority, not a temporary campaign or one-attempt exception. It supersedes previous delivery campaign clocks, attempt allocations, revision ceilings, cumulative local-testing caps, and renewal requirements. Preserve historical accounting and evidence; mark obsolete restrictions superseded rather than deleting history.

This direction governs development working methods. It does not mark any feature complete or authorize unrelated product outcomes. Preserve unrelated owner decisions and protections.

## Own the outcome

Codex owns investigation, implementation, debugging, refactoring, architecture changes, necessary components and development dependencies, testing, installation, and demonstration of the agreed outcome.

A blocker is work to resolve, not an automatic stop point. Investigate causes, repair underlying problems, verify corrections, try appropriate alternatives, and continue. Do not repeat unchanged failures indefinitely.

Implementation scope may expand as necessary to deliver the agreed product outcome. A larger repair, new component, new candidate revision, or failed attempt does not by itself require an owner decision.

Do not restrict work to tiny patches or narrow repair envelopes. Do not require the owner to coordinate tools, relay prompts, supervise routine cleanup, or renew ordinary engineering permission.

## Operational controls, not delivery caps

Choose and adjust practical operation timeouts, concurrency, retries, resource monitoring, and retention independently. Detect hangs and runaway activity without turning an individual timeout into termination of the assignment.

Retain usage accounting. Respect actual service quotas, available hardware, system permissions, and billing arrangements. Campaign caps must not be recreated as application gates or artificial cumulative allowances.

Checkpoints and progress reports are not stop points. If a session or platform limit interrupts work, preserve a resumable handoff and checkpoint under this same authority.

## Meaningful protections

Preserve credentials, unrelated work, original source, task history, important evidence, and usable recovery copies. Keep independent verification; repair defective checks without weakening their intended protection.

Use authorized administrative and service-control mechanisms. Investigate supported alternatives when privileges are unavailable; do not bypass system access controls.

Do not introduce new paid spending, purchase credits, enable automatic billing reloads, or change subscriptions without authorization. Existing included usage remains subject to actual service availability and quotas.

Keep the approved local/private-network exposure boundary and model-service authentication. Respect source-disclosure permissions: local read access is not permission to upload additional private source externally.

Publishing, pushing, merging, external pull requests, and changes to the live game addon require the relevant authorization. Development authority is not blanket publication authority.

Use proportionate verification, deployment, and recovery practices. Do not make safety procedures into a separate enterprise process that prevents useful delivery.

## Ask the owner only when necessary

First investigate supported ways to resolve an obstacle independently. Ask only for a genuinely ambiguous product decision, unavailable access, new paid spending, a consequential action outside existing authorization, or a material change to the intended product outcome.

Explain the concrete boundary, what was tried, and the specific decision or access needed. Combine foreseeable requests where practical.

Do not ask for another time extension, attempt allocation, revision allowance, or routine debugging permission.

## Completion and continuity

Completion requires the agreed useful workflow to be demonstrated in the running application. Passing tests, generating a plan, improving diagnostics, or building a candidate is supporting evidence, not product completion.

Keep uncompleted requirements visible. Maintain concise current-status and handoff records, with distinctions among built, simulated, real-model-tested, installed, and demonstrated results.

Perplexity must carry this standing direction into future assignments and must not recreate superseded restrictions. Codex should reconcile conflicting saved instructions and application controls as part of its work, preserving unrelated protections and historical evidence.
